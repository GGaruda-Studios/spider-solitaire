Privacy Policy

Spider Solitaire Classic · Effective 11 September 2026

In short: we have no server and no account system, and we never see your game. Everything the app remembers — your game in progress, your statistics, and your chosen theme — stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

The app does show ads, supplied by Google AdMob. Serving an ad means Google receives data from your device, including your Android advertising ID and your IP address. That is described in full below, and it is the only way any data leaves your device.

Who this policy covers

Spider Solitaire Classic (the "App") is developed and published by Golden Garuda Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App handles and how, for anyone who installs or uses it.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. We have no server, no analytics service and no account system, so there is nothing on our side to receive, store or see. We do not know who you are, that you installed the App, or how you play it.

That is not the same as saying no data leaves your device. It does — to Google, for advertising, and to nobody else. See Advertising below.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue a game later and see your own statistics, the App stores the following data locally on your device only, using Android's standard app-private storage:

  • Your game in progress — the board layout, score, and elapsed time, so you can resume where you left off.
  • Your career statistics — games played, games won, streaks, and level/XP progress.
  • Your preferences — chosen background, card face, card back, sound/vibration, and other in-app settings.

None of this ever leaves your device. It is private to the App, is not readable by other apps, and is permanently deleted the moment you uninstall the App or clear its storage from Android's Settings.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. There are two places they appear: a banner strip at the bottom of the game screen, and a short video you may choose to watch in exchange for a joker. The video never plays unless you ask for it by pressing the joker button.

To serve an ad, Google's software receives information from your device. According to Google, this can include:

  • your Android advertising ID, a resettable identifier used to limit repeated ads and to measure them;
  • your IP address, which indicates your approximate location — typically your country or region, not your precise location;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • ad interaction data — that an ad was shown, and whether it was viewed or tapped.

This information goes to Google, not to us. We receive only anonymous, aggregate totals, such as how many ads were shown — never anything about an individual person or device. How Google handles it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The App uses no other third-party SDK. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no social network integration and no tracking of any other kind.

Your consent, and how to change it

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before any personalised advertising, using Google's consent tool. You may refuse, in which case you will see non-personalised ads instead — ads chosen without using the data above to build a profile of you. Refusing restricts no part of the game.

Wherever you are, Android gives you direct control over the advertising ID. Under Settings → Privacy → Ads — the exact path varies by device — you can reset or delete it and turn off ad personalisation entirely. Deleting it stops apps, including this one, from using it at all.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Its target audience on Google Play is 13 and over.

This changed when advertising was added. An earlier version of this policy described the App as suitable for all ages, and it was — it had no way to collect anything from anyone. Serving ads means device-level data now reaches Google, so that claim is no longer accurate, and we have withdrawn it rather than leave it standing.

The game itself is unchanged and contains nothing unsuitable for a younger player. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and you have a concern, contact us at the address below.

Your choices

Everything the App stores — your game, your statistics, your preferences — lives only on your device, so you are always in full control of it: clear the App's storage or uninstall the App from Android's Settings at any time to remove it completely. There is no account to delete and no request to send us, because we hold nothing about you.

For the advertising data described above, your controls are your consent choice and the Android advertising ID settings, both covered in Your consent, and how to change it.

Changes to this policy
