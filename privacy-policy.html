Privacy Policy

Spider Solitaire Classic · Golden Garuda Studios · Effective 13 September 2026

The short version. We have no server, no accounts and no analytics, so we never see you or your game. Your saved game, your statistics and your settings are written to your own device and stay there.

The one thing that leaves your device is advertising. The game shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, and serving an ad means Google receives data from your device — your advertising ID and IP address among it. That is set out in full below. Nothing else is sent anywhere, and nothing at all is sent to us.

1. Who we are

Spider Solitaire Classic (the “App”) is developed and published by Golden Garuda Studios (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the App handles, where it goes, and what you can do about it. It applies to the App only.

For the small amount of data that does leave your device, we are responsible for the decision to include advertising at all; Google is responsible for what its advertising systems then do with that data, under its own policy, linked in section 4.

2. What we collect about you

Nothing. We operate no server, no database, no analytics service, no crash reporting and no account system. There is nothing on our side to receive your data, store it, or look at it. We do not know your name, your email address, that you installed the App, or how you play it.

From advertising we receive only anonymous, aggregated totals through Google's AdMob dashboard — how many ads were shown and what they earned — never anything that identifies a person or a device.

3. What the App stores on your device

So that you can put the game down and pick it up again, the App writes the following to its own private storage on your device:

WhatWhy
Your game in progress — board, score, moves and elapsed timeSo an interrupted game is still there when you come back
Your statistics — games played and won, streaks, level and XPSo your own record survives between sessions
Your settings — background, card face, card back, difficulty, sound, vibration, orientation and the restSo the game looks and behaves the way you left it

This is app-private storage: other apps cannot read it, we cannot read it, and it is never uploaded. It is deleted the moment you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. There is no cloud save and no account, and the App switches Android's automatic backup off, so this data is not copied to your Google account either.

4. Advertising

The App shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, in two places:

  • a banner strip at the bottom of the game screen;
  • a short video, offered only when you press the joker button. It never plays unless you ask for it.

To select and measure an ad, Google's software inside the App sends information from your device to Google. According to Google, this can include:

  • your Android advertising ID — a resettable identifier, not tied to your name, used to limit repeat ads and to count them;
  • your IP address, which indicates approximate location, normally at the level of a country or region rather than a precise place;
  • device information such as model, operating system version, screen size and language;
  • ad interaction data — that an ad was shown, and whether it was watched or tapped.

This goes to Google, not to us, and Google may transfer and store it outside the country you are in. What Google does with it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The App contains no other third-party SDK. No analytics, no crash reporting, no social network, no attribution or tracking service of any kind.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising beyond what is described here. We have never been paid for data, and our only revenue from the App is Google's share of what these ads earn.

5. Your consent, and changing your mind

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your choices before any personalised advertising, using Google's User Messaging Platform — the standard consent form you will have seen in other apps. Depending on what you choose you will see personalised ads, non-personalised ads, or no ads at all. Refusing costs you nothing in the game: every feature, jokers included, works exactly the same either way.

Wherever you live, Android itself gives you control of the advertising ID. Under Settings → Privacy → Ads — the exact path varies by device and Android version — you can reset it, turn off ad personalisation, or delete the ID entirely, which stops apps, this one included, from using it at all.

6. Your rights

Data protection law in many places gives you the right to see, correct, delete or export the personal data a company holds about you, and to object to its being used. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase and no request for you to send us. In practice:

  • Data on your device is yours and under your direct control — clear the App's storage or uninstall the App to erase all of it.
  • Data Google receives from advertising is handled under Google's own policy, and your controls over it are your consent choice and the Android advertising ID settings described above.

If you think we have handled something wrongly, write to us at the address in section 9. You are also entitled to complain to the data protection authority where you live.

7. Children

The game itself is suitable for everyone and is content-rated accordingly: a card game with no violence, no chat, no user-generated content and no gambling.

The audience we declare the App for on Google Play is 13 and over, which is a separate matter from that content rating. The reason is advertising. The App serves ordinary AdMob ads and uses the Android advertising ID, and that is not a configuration we are prepared to aim at children. The App is therefore not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them — as section 2 says, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child's use of the App, contact us and we will help however we can.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page and the effective date at the top changes with it. If a change is a significant one — a new kind of data leaving your device, say — we will say so in the App's release notes rather than let it pass quietly. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
