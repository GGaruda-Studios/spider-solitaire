Privacy Policy

Spider Solitaire Classic · Effective 4 September 2026

In short: Spider Solitaire Classic does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no account system, requests no Internet permission, and does not connect to any server. Everything the app remembers — your game in progress, your statistics, and your chosen theme — stays on your device.

Who this policy covers

Spider Solitaire Classic (the "App") is developed and published by Golden Garuda Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App handles and how, for anyone who installs or uses it.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information about you. The App does not request the Internet permission and cannot send data anywhere, even if it wanted to. We have no server, no analytics service, and no account system, so there is nothing on our side to receive, store, or see.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue a game later and see your own statistics, the App stores the following data locally on your device only, using Android's standard app-private storage:

  • Your game in progress — the board layout, score, and elapsed time, so you can resume where you left off.
  • Your career statistics — games played, games won, streaks, and level/XP progress.
  • Your preferences — chosen background, card face, card back, sound/vibration, and other in-app settings.

None of this ever leaves your device. It is private to the App, is not readable by other apps, and is permanently deleted the moment you uninstall the App or clear its storage from Android's Settings.

Third parties

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs at this time. It shares no data with anyone, because it collects none to share.

If a future version of the App introduces advertising or in-app purchases, those features will bring their own, clearly-disclosed data practices (for example, an ad network's own privacy policy), and this page will be updated before that version is released — not after.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for players of all ages, including children, and is designed with that in mind: it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, because it has no way to collect information at all — no account system, no Internet permission, no analytics, and no advertising. There is nothing about a child's use of the App for us, or anyone else, to see or store.

This complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy by design, not by policy alone. If a future update introduces advertising or in-app purchases, we will ensure any such feature meets the applicable requirements for an app suitable for children before it ships, and this page will be updated accordingly.

Your choices

Because everything the App stores lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it: clear the App's storage or uninstall the App from Android's Settings at any time to remove it completely. There is no account to delete and no request to send us, since we hold nothing.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
